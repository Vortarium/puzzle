VORTARIUM

          
Rules

          
01
Prize
A $1,500 prize awaits the first person to complete all 100 cryptography puzzles and pass the final verification process.

          
02
Time & resources
You have unlimited time and may use any resources available to you, including:
  • AI assistants and chatbots
  • Friends, family, and experts
  • Books, online resources, and research materials
  • Any tools that help you solve the puzzles

          
03
Age requirement & parental consent
If you are under 18, you need parental or guardian consent to claim the prize. Your parent or legal guardian must contact Vortarium / Logan Sattler to verify consent before the prize can be awarded.

          
04
Fair-play & verification
Extracting or reverse-engineering source code to skip levels or obtain answers is strictly prohibited. At the end of the challenge, Vortarium / Logan Sattler may require you to demonstrate every answer with your working, reasoning, and relevant intermediate steps. Multi-step puzzles must be supported by your process.

          
05
Progress & data
Your progress, notebook, and canvas are saved in this browser on this device. Use Save work regularly to create an encrypted, password-protected backup. We are not responsible for lost progress caused by cleared browser data, device changes, or a lost backup password.

          
06
Puzzle instructions & answers
Every instruction for every level appears inside the puzzle screen. There are no hidden answers elsewhere in the interface. All answers are case-sensitive and must be typed exactly as the puzzle specifies.
